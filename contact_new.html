Frequently Asked Questions

How can I update my profile information?

You can update your profile information by logging into your account and navigating to the profile section. Click on the edit button to modify your details.

How do I register for alumni events?

To register for alumni events, log in to your account and visit the Events page. Browse through the upcoming events and click on the "Register" button for the event you wish to attend.

How can I access the alumni directory?

The alumni directory is accessible to registered members. Log in to your account and click on the "Directory" link in the navigation menu to browse through the alumni database.

How can I contribute to the alumni portal?

You can contribute to the alumni portal by sharing your success stories, participating in events, mentoring current students, or making donations to support various initiatives.
